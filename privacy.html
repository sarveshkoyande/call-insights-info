Privacy Policy — Call Insights

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the extension handles

Call Insights reads the live captions shown in your Microsoft Teams meeting tab (speaker names and caption text) to build a transcript and in-call insights. This data is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your own computer.

Google user data

  • What we access: only with your permission, using the drive.file scope. This lets the extension create files and a folder in your Google Drive and access only those files. It cannot see, read, change or delete any other files in your Drive. It also reads your Google account email to show which account is connected.
  • How we use it: to save meeting transcripts you choose to send as text files in a "Call Insights" folder in your own Drive.
  • Storage: the Google access token and your account email are stored only in your browser's local extension storage. They are not sent to us or any other server.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Files go directly from your browser to your Google Drive.
  • Removing access: click Disconnect in the extension, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Call Insights' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services you configure

If you turn on optional features, transcript text is sent to the service you set up: the AI coach / meeting-minutes provider you select (for example Anthropic, Google Gemini, or your own endpoint), or a streaming URL you enter (for example a Power Automate flow). Those services handle the data under their own policies.
